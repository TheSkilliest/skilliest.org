Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a Master's degree to apply?

+

No! A Master's degree is NOT required. In fact, programs in the US normally do not require a Master's degree for a direct PhD program—you can secure a PhD directly from a Bachelor's! I secured multiple fully-funded PhD offers with just a Bachelor's degree. While a Master's is a plus if you already have one, it is absolutely not necessary. I am living proof that the direct Bachelor's-to-PhD route is completely achievable.

What if I don't have a strong research profile?

+

We help you build and present your profile in the absolute best possible light. We mentor you to make your CV the "best of the best," build a highly professional online presence (like LinkedIn and a personal academic website), define your research interests, and position yourself strategically to stand out to faculty.

How is this different from traditional consultants?

+

Traditional consultants give generic advice without execution systems. They do not help you apply for free to programs, nor do they help you optimize your long-term costs—such as advising you on exactly which English tests to take, when to take them, or how to send your scores cheaply. We provide an execution-focused bootcamp. We mentor you on setting up AI-powered automation, exact email frameworks, and automated follow-up systems. We actually care about your wallet and show you how to take action, not just give you theory.

How do fee waivers work, and can I really apply for free?

+

Yes! We help you apply for free to programs in the US and worldwide that normally require expensive application fees. We help you secure numerous application fee waivers because we have the exact expertise on who to contact, how to contact them, and how to successfully apply for and receive these waivers. We know the insider strategies to bypass these costs, saving you a fortune during your application journey.

What is the best part of this outreach framework?

+

The best part of our framework is that it provides you the strength and capacity to explore every opportunity all around the world. It comprehensively covers both applying to officially posted/advertised PhD positions AND aggressively contacting professors directly to uncover hidden funding.

Am I buying a custom software from you?

+

No. It’s not a software. We teach you a proven framework and execution strategy. We show you exactly how to link existing, everyday tools—like Gmail, Google Sheets, and AI APIs—to create an automated outreach machine based on our battle-tested methods. It is a powerful methodology that puts you in complete control and ahead of all other applicants.

How does the automatic AI-based cold emailing work?

+

Using our framework, the emails are written in bulk by artificial intelligence and then sent automatically by the automation system. With just a few clicks, you can generate and send your outreach. This system gives you the incredible strength to seamlessly send hundreds—or even thousands—of emails, completely replacing hundreds of hours of manual labor with a high-speed, automated execution machine.

If I am sending emails in bulk, how is every email personalized?

+

Our framework involves the automatic collection of each professor's specific research interests. The AI uses this exact data to write highly customized and personalized cold emails that speak directly to that individual professor's work. This gives you a massive edge over other applicants: you gain the sheer volume and strength of sending hundreds or thousands of emails, while maintaining the deep, one-on-one personalization that professors look for.

How does the automated follow-up system work?

+

Using our framework and strategy, the AI automatically writes context-aware follow-up emails for every initial email you sent. You stay in complete control while the system periodically and automatically follows up with every professor you've contacted, keeping the messaging highly personalized.

Is the automated follow-up system really that important?

+

Yes! It is the MOST CRITICAL part of the entire process. Most students send one single email and give up. Professors are incredibly busy people; your email often just gets buried. Following up automatically every few days dramatically increases your response rate. Furthermore, funding isn't always readily available the exact moment you send your first email. You have to stay on their radar until a funded position opens up. Consistent, automated follow-ups are exactly what converts cold emails into actual interviews and funded offers.

Can I really send unlimited emails?

+

Yes! Because you own the setup of this framework, your capacity is practically unlimited. You can easily send up to 500 emails per day for free using standard systems. This allows you to try every possible opportunity across the globe, ensuring you never regret not trying.

Which countries and universities can I target?

+

Any country! The US, Canada, Australia, UK, Europe, the Middle East, and beyond. This outreach framework works globally. You will learn how to efficiently target professors and programs anywhere in the world, connecting with thousands of faculty members.

Is there a specific time of year to start?

+

No, because that's not how funding works! Funding comes and goes all year round. A professor can secure a new grant and offer a fully-funded position at any time. Even though university intakes are typically Fall, Spring, or Summer, there is no "wrong" time to contact a professor. The key is to get in touch and stay on their radar using our follow-up system.

Will I be left on my own if I get stuck?

+

Absolutely not. We won't let you get stuck anywhere. You will have our back through the entire process, step-by-step. Whenever you hit a roadblock, we are there to guide you through it.

Do you help with Master's applications?

+

Yes! While this specific bootcamp is exclusively designed for securing fully-funded PhD positions, we absolutely help Master's applicants. I personally navigated the Master's application process and successfully secured around 20 admissions to top Master's programs across the US. Because the bootcamp's automated framework is optimized for PhD outreach, we offer dedicated, 1-on-1 personalized consultation services specifically tailored to help Master's applicants achieve the same level of success.

Which academic fields or majors do you cover?

+

We cover almost every field! Whether you are in STEM (Engineering, Computer Science, Biology), Humanities, Social Sciences, or Business, our mass-outreach framework works perfectly for every area of study. The core mechanics of collecting professor data, using AI to write highly personalized emails based on research interests, and setting up automated follow-ups are universal strategies that maximize success across all academic disciplines.

+

Applications are open right now. Because this is an exclusive action-taking bootcamp, we start as soon as we shortlist applicants for our highly limited slots. The bootcamp is conducted directly by me and features bootcamp sessions and dedicated 1-on-1 support. We know you have other commitments, so the live sessions are highly flexible. The exact days and timings will be set entirely through the mutual consensus of all selected applicants.

+

Because you'll never regret trying! Our automated email framework allows you to explore every possible opportunity without the traditional limitations of time and burnout.
