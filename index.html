My Journey

Hi, I'm Waleed Abdullah

I am an Electrical Engineer from Lahore, Pakistan, and I navigated the entire graduate application journey—from Master's applications to securing multiple fully-funded PhD offers in the US.

🎓 All achieved with just a Bachelor's degree – No Master's degree required!

2023

Master's Applications Journey

Applied to 15+ top MS programs in the US and got accepted to almost all of them:

University of Washington University of Michigan at Ann Arbor Pennsylvania State University North Carolina State University Ohio State University Virginia Tech University Northeastern University Illinois Institute of Technology University of Delaware University of North Dakota

Key Realization: Master's programs cost ~$120K with minimal funding. Decided to pursue a fully-funded PhD directly from my Bachelor's degree instead – and proved it's absolutely possible!

2024

First PhD Success (Bachelor's to PhD Direct)

Secured a fully-funded PhD offer in Electrical Engineering at a top US university for Fall 2025 – directly from my Bachelor's degree, no Master's required!

Challenge: Funding was impacted at the last moment—a critical lesson in not relying on a single offer.

2025

Aggressive PhD Campaign

Contacted almost all relevant professors worldwide, majorly in the US

  • 1000+ professors contacted globally
  • 30+ PhD programs applied (with fee waivers)
  • Multiple PhD offers secured
  • All with just a Bachelor's degree

Diversification: Also applied to fully-funded programs in Europe and KSA as backup options.

2026

Current Status

  • 20+ PhD applications still active
  • Numerous PhD offers received
  • Accepted and proceeding with the best offer
  • Still receiving interview requests from professors
  • All accomplished with a Bachelor's degree only

Hard-Earned Lessons

❌ What Didn't Work

  • Generic consultants who didn't mention fee waivers or professor outreach
  • Spending on official test scores to every school (~$25/institution)
  • Relying on a single PhD offer
  • Not following up systematically
  • Not trying every possible opportunity

✅ What Actually Worked

  • Aggressive professor outreach worldwide (1000+ emails)
  • Automated follow-up system (most critical)
  • Securing multiple fee waivers
  • Diversifying applications across countries
  • Cost optimization at every step
  • Trying every possible chance – never giving up!

Amount I Spent Over 2+ Years

$6,000+

Includes multiple test attempts (TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo), application fees, test score sending, and more. Most of this could have been optimized with the right guidance from the start.
